x2Board 私隱政策

生效日期：2026 年 9 月 27 日

x2Board 係一個 Android 鍵盤。我哋嘅原則好簡單：你打嘅字留喺你部手機。x2Board 冇帳戶、冇廣告、冇分析或者追蹤工具，亦都唔會出售任何資料。

留喺手機嘅資料

  • 學習字詞：為咗建議字詞，x2Board 會記住你揀過嘅中文字詞、打過嘅英文生字、電郵地址同用戶名。無痕模式、密碼欄同標記為敏感嘅欄位唔會學習。
  • 剪貼簿記錄：儲存最近複製嘅文字，方便再貼上（可以喺設定關閉）；標記為敏感嘅內容（例如密碼）唔會記錄。
  • 文字替代、設定：你自己加嘅捷徑同設定。

以上資料只儲存喺 x2Board 嘅私人空間，唔會上載。你可以喺設定清除已學字詞、喺剪貼簿面板清除記錄，或者解除安裝 x2Board 刪除全部資料。

會經網絡傳送嘅資料（只喺你主動使用時）

  • ✨ 寫作工具（AI）：預設關閉。開啟並撳某個 AI 功能（例如校對、改寫、翻譯）時，你揀咗嘅文字會經加密連線（HTTPS）傳送去 x2 伺服器處理，然後將結果傳返畀你。x2 伺服器會使用第三方 AI 服務供應商生成結果，供應商會按佢自己嘅私隱政策處理文字。同時會傳送一個喺你部手機隨機產生嘅 ID（唔係你嘅帳戶、電話號碼或者裝置序號），只係用嚟計每部機每日最多 100 次；伺服器只會記住當日嘅次數，第二日就刪除。x2 伺服器唔會儲存或者記錄你嘅文字。唯一例外係你喺結果頁撳「檢舉」：嗰次嘅原文同 AI 結果會傳送去 x2 伺服器，用嚟檢查同改善 AI，保留最多 90 日，唔會連同任何 ID。格式化 JSON / HTML 只喺手機做；密碼欄唔會提供寫作工具。
  • 🎤 聽寫：撳 🎤 時，錄音交畀你手機內置嘅語音辨識服務（通常係 Google）轉做文字。x2Board 本身唔會儲存或者上載錄音。
  • 手寫辨識：第一次用手寫時會由 Google 下載辨識模型；之後辨識喺手機上完成，筆跡唔會上載。
  • 自動填入：密碼、驗證碼同地址建議由你揀嘅 Android 自動填入服務（例如 Google）提供，x2Board 只係顯示。

權限

  • 咪高峰：只用嚟聽寫。
  • 網絡：只用嚟做上面列出嘅 AI 寫作工具同下載手寫模型。
  • 震動：按鍵觸覺回饋。

兒童

x2Board 唔係專為 13 歲以下兒童而設，亦唔會刻意收集兒童資料。

政策更新同聯絡

如果呢份政策有改動，我哋會更新呢一頁同上面嘅生效日期。有任何問題，請經 Google Play 上 x2Board 頁面嘅開發者電郵聯絡我哋。

x2Board Privacy Policy

Effective 27 September 2026

x2Board is an Android keyboard built on one principle: what you type stays on your phone. x2Board has no accounts, no ads and no analytics or tracking, and it never sells any data.

Data kept on your phone

  • Learned words: to make suggestions, x2Board remembers Chinese words you pick, new English words, email addresses and usernames you type. Nothing is learned in incognito fields, password fields or fields marked sensitive.
  • Clipboard history: recent copies, so you can paste them again (can be turned off in Settings). Copies marked sensitive (such as passwords) are skipped.
  • Text replacements and settings that you create.

This data is stored only in x2Board's private app storage and is never uploaded. You can clear learned words in Settings and clipboard history in the clipboard panel, or uninstall x2Board to delete everything.

Data sent over the network, only when you use a feature

  • ✨ Writing Tools (AI): off by default. When it is on and you tap an AI action (for example proofread, rewrite or translate), the selected text is sent over an encrypted HTTPS connection to the x2 server for processing, and the result is returned to you. The x2 server uses a third-party AI service provider to produce the result, which handles the text under its own privacy policy. A random ID made on your phone is sent with it (not your account, phone number or device serial), only to allow each phone up to 100 actions a day; the server keeps only today's count and deletes it the next day. The x2 server does not store or log your text. The only exception is when you tap Report on a result: that text and the AI result are sent to the x2 server to review and improve the AI, kept for up to 90 days and not linked to any ID. JSON / HTML formatting runs on the phone, and Writing Tools is never offered in password fields.
  • 🎤 Dictation: when you tap 🎤, audio goes to your phone's built-in speech recognition service (usually Google) to be turned into text. x2Board itself does not store or upload recordings.
  • Handwriting: the recognition model is downloaded from Google the first time you use handwriting. Recognition then runs on the phone, and your handwriting is not uploaded.
  • Autofill: password, code and address suggestions come from the Android autofill service you chose (such as Google). x2Board only displays them.

Permissions

  • Microphone: dictation only.
  • Internet: only for the AI Writing Tools and downloading the handwriting model listed above.
  • Vibrate: haptic feedback on key presses.

Children

x2Board is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect children's data.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, this page and the effective date above will be updated. For any question, contact us through the developer email on x2Board's Google Play page.
